Marlborough

Every local number the desk holds for this town, each naming its own source. Know where you live.

Marlborough's net grand list grew 37.9% between the latest two assessment rolls, 16th-highest of 169 Connecticut places. Its FY2027 mill rate is 27.10 mills per $1,000 of taxable value, 1.02× the statewide average. The Census Bureau's 2024 ACS 5-year (2020–2024) estimate puts median household income at $142,105 and 9.7% of residents below poverty.
