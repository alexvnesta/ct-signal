Bristol's net grand list grew 1.6% between the latest two assessment rolls, 69th-highest of 169 Connecticut places. Its FY2027 mill rate is 35.30 mills per $1,000 of taxable value, 1.32× the statewide average. Single-family homes sold for a median of $330,000 in 2024 (609 recorded sales). The Census Bureau's 2024 ACS 5-year (2020–2024) estimate puts median household income at $79,076 and 10.7% of residents below poverty.
