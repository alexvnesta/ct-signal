What changed

The latest observation (2025) is $1,679, up $129 from $1,550 (2024).

Why it matters

Rent is a fixed monthly bill, so it decides how much of a paycheck is left for everything else. A state median also shows whether renters are absorbing the cost of a tight housing market or whether new supply is giving them room.
