What changed

The latest observation (2025) is 9.1%, down 1.1 points from 10.2% (2024).

Why it matters

A poverty rate counts people below a fixed national threshold, not a local cost of living. It shows how many households the safety net is meant to reach, while the threshold itself stays the same whether housing is cheap or expensive.
