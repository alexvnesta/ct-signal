What changed

The latest observation (2025) is $423,900, up $27,000 from $396,900 (2024).

Why it matters

Median home value is the middle of the market, not the average. It shapes what a buyer must borrow and what a town can tax, and comparing medians keeps a handful of expensive sales from standing in for the whole state.
