Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no ad trackers.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you personally. CT Signal has no login, no ad network, no third-party tracking pixel, and no cross-site cookies. Reading the site does not create a profile.

Server logs

Our host, Vercel, keeps ordinary request logs for security and reliability. They are not joined to a reader identity and are not used to build an audience profile.

Cookieless measurement

On production (ctsignal.org), we load two first-party Vercel scripts: Vercel Speed Insights (Core Web Vitals: load speed, layout stability, interaction latency) and Vercel Web Analytics (aggregate, cookieless page-path counts and referrers so we know which town and story pages readers actually use). Neither sets cookies, fingerprints browsers, or tracks you across sites.

The weekly email

If you subscribe, Buttondown stores the email address you type and the delivery record for the digest. You can unsubscribe in one click from any issue. We do not sell, rent, or trade the list, and we do not add you to any other list.

What we do not do

  • No advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or data brokers.
  • No cross-site profiling or fingerprinting.
  • No paid placement that changes a number or a ranking.
