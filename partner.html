Partner & underwrite

Clean local underwriting and automated Connecticut data tools — strictly separated from the numbers.

CT Signal publishes live, verifiable public data for all 169 Connecticut municipalities — property tax rates, recorded home sales, housing permits, school spending, and Census benchmarks — with zero LLM-written numbers and zero ad-tech trackers.

1. Underwrite the Monday brief & town desk

Flat-rate quarterly underwriting (NPR / public-media style) for Connecticut credit unions, regional lenders, real estate brokerages, clean-energy providers, universities, and civic organizations who want to reach engaged Connecticut homeowners, relocators, and municipal leaders.

  • What you get: A clearly labeled “Underwritten by” acknowledgment in the Monday weekly brief and across our 169-town reference desk and Compare towns tool.
  • Zero ad tech: Plain text and link — no tracking pixels, no programmatic banners, no third-party cookies.

2. Newsroom embeds & co-branded town briefs

Built for Connecticut newsrooms, hyperlocal newsletters, regional Councils of Governments, civic nonprofits, and real estate relocation teams:

  • Live story embeds & CSVs: Every story and indicator file offers copy-paste chart embeds, 2× PNG graphics, and raw CSV receipts. Free for non-commercial republication with attribution.
  • White-label briefs & custom feeds: Automated weekly briefs, custom multi-town packets, or scheduled data feeds tailored to your region or readership.

Editorial firewall

Underwriting pays for infrastructure and coverage depth — never outcomes. No partner or sponsor can add, remove, round, or influence any dataset, ranking, chart, or correction. Every methodology rule and correction log stays public on GitHub.
