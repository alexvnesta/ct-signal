CT Signal

CTData answers questions.
This makes CTData ask them — and every number is fetched, never generated.
live: ct-signal.vercel.app

The problem: a last mile of attention

  • CTData puts out real data for democracy — 11 topic portals, dashboards, town profiles…
  • …but people only act on data when a story makes them care. That moment passes.
  • Evidence it's real: CTData staffs a human “Ask a Data Question” Data Helpline — people want answers and can't reach them.
  • Our "who": the 99% who'll never open a data portal — and CTData, whose newsletter needs exactly this content.

  • Why AI is integral: news arrives as language; Data Commons speaks DCIDs. Judging which story is a population-level story, and which indicator answers it — semantic intent and relevance — is exactly the step rules can't do.
  • But the LLM never writes a number: its judgment picks the query; official APIs return the answer. Failure mode is a missing card, never a wrong one.

Impact — and receipts

  • Drops straight into CTData's weekly newsletter, homepage, or an SMS line — per-town and multilingual are config changes, not rebuilds.
  • Validated live, failures included: BLS v1 dead, portal search leaked other states, a "voter turnout" dataset was a hyperlink. The log ships in the repo.
  • Next: SOTUS election CSVs (Nov 3), more datasets = more card types. The whole surface area is one YAML file.
