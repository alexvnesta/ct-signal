The problem

CTData's numbers arrive too late to the conversation

  • 11 portals, a metrics report, a county index — all of it waiting for people who already know to look.
  • People engage when a story breaks — and by the time the data arrives, the moment is gone.
  • CTData already staffs a manual "Ask a Data Question" line. Demand exists. The pipeline doesn't.
