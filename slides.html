The problem: a last mile of attention

  • CTData puts out real data for democracy — 11 topic portals, dashboards, town profiles…
  • …but people only act on data when a story makes them care. That moment passes.
  • Evidence it's real: CTData staffs a human “Ask a Data Question” Data Helpline — people want answers and can't reach them.
  • Our "who": the 99% who'll never open a data portal — and CTData, whose newsletter needs exactly this content.

Impact — and receipts

  • Drops into CTData's existing weekly newsletter, homepage, or an SMS line; multilingual and per-town are config changes, not rebuilds.
  • Every endpoint, DCID and dataset was validated live — including what failed: BLS v1 is dead, portal search leaked other states' data, a "voter turnout" dataset turned out to be a hyperlink. The log ships in the repo.
  • Next: SOTUS election CSVs (Nov 3), town digest emails, more datasets = more card types — catalog/indicators.yaml is the whole surface area.
