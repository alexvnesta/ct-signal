How many Connecticut residents live below the poverty line compared with the rest of the country?

Published October 5, 2026, 4:28 PM EDT · CT Signal automated data desk
10.2% of Connecticut residents live below the poverty line (2024), the 15th-lowest of 52 peers.

Half the peer group sits under 11.6%. Connecticut is 1.4 points below the middle.
