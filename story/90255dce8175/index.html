How does Connecticut’s reported violent crime compare with the rest of the country?

Published October 6, 2026, 8:57 AM EDT · CT Signal automated data desk
Connecticut reports 147 violent crimes per 100k people (2023), the 3rd-lowest reported of 52 peers. Reported rates compare reporting, not safety.

Half the peer group sits under 325 crimes. Connecticut is 178 crimes below the middle.
