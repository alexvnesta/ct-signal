What changed

The latest observation (October 2026) is 5.1%, unchanged from 5.1% (August 2026).

Why it matters

The unemployment rate counts people who are actively looking for work, not everyone without a job. It can fall because hiring picked up or because discouraged workers stopped searching, so the rate is a start for a labor-market story, not the whole story.
